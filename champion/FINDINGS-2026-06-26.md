   possession ticks (replay of live FCTICK), our PASS format matches the official AWS sample exactly
   (_build/sample-ai-possibilities/.../lib/fallback.py), and possession-transition analysis shows the
   holder pid changes between our 5 players 38/101 transitions → passes DO complete. PASS=0 counts something
   other than accepted-commands. Do NOT re-chase PASS format. (Cost this session: ~6 deploy cycles.)
3. **Structural defensive diagnosis (live FCTICK, 87 opp-attack-our-third ticks):** only ~1.08 of our 4
   outfielders are goal-side of the ball; 34% of those ticks have an attacker behind our ENTIRE line (a free
   shooter). attack-always 1-1-2 has near-zero transition defence. This is mechanistically WHY aggressive
   (which floods that space) coin-flips us while balanced/defensive (which don't) get shut out 4-0/6-0.
4. **Underpowered-A/B reality (operator's correct meta-critique):** this game's single-match variance is huge
   (±6 goals). At n=12-16, win% CI ≈ [22,71]; power to detect +20%p ≈ 8%. Signals were CONTRADICTORY between
   configs (e.g. opp_on_target went 2.75 with recovery but 3.9 without). Only a lever with a BIG net effect is
   measurable here; micro-tuning is below the noise floor. Judge defensive levers on opp_on_target (lowest
   variance), but even that flipped sign between configs → treat all single-config conclusions as weak.
